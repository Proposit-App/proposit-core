# v1.5.1 release notes

## Bug Fixes

- Claims extracted from text are no longer prefixed with an attributive
  wrapper such as "The author claims that…", "The author argues…", or
  "According to the author…". Ingestion now emits the proposition
  itself as a plain declarative sentence (e.g. "Rain wets the ground"
  instead of "The author claims that rain wets the ground"). Citation
  claims still summarize what their external cited source says — that
  attribution is intentional and unchanged.
